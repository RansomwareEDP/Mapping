READ THESE NUMBERS CAREFULLY:
 Absence is not innocence: zero means nothing was REPORTED on that network, and reporting is uneven and volunteer-driven. Indicators on compromised machines are counted separately and must never be added to the rest, because a hacked server is a victim, not inventory.
