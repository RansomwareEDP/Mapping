READ THESE NUMBERS CAREFULLY:
 The API returns at most 1000 samples per tag, newest first, so any tag marked truncated yields FLOORS, not counts, and cannot support a claim that usage rose or fell. Counts also track what gets submitted to MalwareBazaar, which shifts when a large contributor joins or leaves. And a wrapper is invisible until somebody writes a tag for it, so time-to-appearance after a takedown measures analyst attention more than adversary behaviour.
