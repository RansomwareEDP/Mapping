EDP Node Intelligence Modules (Reference by Node):

The 15 EDP modules provide deep-dive intelligence on each node in the Dependency Map. Each module follows a standard 8-section structure: definition, key actors, infrastructure dependencies, disruption leverage points, resilience, KPIs, sources, and analyst assessment. Consult the relevant module before taking action on a node or preparing a node-level briefing.
