01

What This Is

This page supplements the Luna Moth / SRG threat actor profile (last updated August 2026) with intelligence drawn from a leak of the group's internal operations chat. The leak surfaced publicly on 7 October 2026. It comprises 5,692 messages across two Rocket.Chat servers (aim6umein3kee.com and slplsskdj2836v2jjsx.com) spanning 27 August 2025 to 29 September 2026. The base profile predates the leak and contains none of this material.

Provenance and confidence. The source is the group's own operational channel, not vendor telemetry. Financial figures are the crew's internal declarations, not confirmed receipts, and are labeled accordingly throughout. Quotes are analyst translations from Russian. Confidence is marked per claim: Confirmed (external instrument), Credible (multiple internal corroboration), Inference (analyst). The group has denied being breached and disputes the leak's origin (8 October 2026).

The Two Principals: Henry and Adrian

The leadership is two co-principals who address each other as "Brat" (brother) and divide the business consistently across the year: Adrian conceives and assigns, Henry builds, pays, and calls. Both operate on the primary server only, in a UTC+1 to UTC+3 diurnal window, supporting a hypothesis that the leadership is co-located or timezone-aligned (Inference).

H
Henry: operator, paymaster, caller

Operations lead and sole treasurer. He posts the wallets, runs payroll, buys infrastructure, and personally cold-calls victims. The single highest-value profiling target in the leak: 3,382 messages across 112 active days, the most of anyone.

  • Residence (Credible, country is Inference): Russian national living in an unnamed African country, not Russia. Self-described markers: pervasive corruption, heavy Chinese construction, British colonial history, highland climate (15 to 24C), direct flights to Moscow via Ethiopia. Points to a former-British, highland East or Southern African country; not positively identified.
  • Assets: two 2026 Moscow apartment purchases (one on Paveletskaya embankment, 85 sqm, terrace, ~$1M, under construction; one on the outskirts for rental), bought by power of attorney, source of funds told to the bank as "savings." A jeep and a local business with fellow Russian expats in the African country.
  • Tradecraft: GrapheneOS on Google Pixel, avoids iPhone; Session Messenger and Tox; per-firm Google Voice burners; non-extradition travel only (avoids Dubai, Turkey, Egypt); Sber Premier and Tinkoff banking.
  • Strongest behavioral tell: calls people "бро" (bro), 129 uses, versus Adrian 0. He does not use "Братик." A payments-running Russian speaker who writes "бро" (not "брат") is a strong Henry candidate.
  • Other tells: pastes AI-assistant output into chat leaving "utm_source=chatgpt.com" URLs; handles the service contacts FAKELAB, Jamel Livingston, Goldbear; recurring Porsche 911 fixation.
  • Crypto: posted 63 of the 65 BTC addresses in the corpus. Attribution is a Data Solutions task, not asserted here.
A
Adrian: strategist, scheme architect

Originates and drives the expansion schemes and administers agent onboarding; delegates execution and money to Henry. 1,668 messages across 96 active days. Discloses almost nothing about himself, an OPSEC asymmetry that is itself a signal.

  • Authorship (Credible): wrote the naval-base Marine-recruitment / nuclear-submarine plan (20 May 2026); proposed and chartered the armed enforcement cell (from 21 Mar 2026); originated the honeypot concept (13 Mar 2026) and the private-investigator tasking (9 May 2026).
  • Direction: sets targets ("plan 350kk this season"), assigns Henry standing tasks, and sets Henry's cut.
  • Behavioral tell: uses "Брат / Братик" (brat / bratik), the inverse of Henry. A clean two-way discriminator. Coins the scheme codenames (PUNISH_POWER_GROUP, HOE_BLACKMAIL, GAY_MARINES_PUBS, CA Adaptation).
  • Crypto: posted only 3 BTC addresses, consistent with his non-treasurer role. One, bc1qty6fppk4k475k64qahredmy0lrhwv00geqjnea, was also posted by Henry one day earlier. That shared wallet is the strongest link between the two identities and a Data Solutions priority.
Do not conflate. "M" is a separate senior operator who runs TRG calling. "Sam" and "Kasatkin" are predecessors discussed as arrested (linked by the operators to the Conti lineage); treat as separate identities and as leads to real arrest records, not established fact.

The wider crew (roles per Ransom-ISAC, 26 Oct 2026; message volumes corroborated here). Beyond the two principals, five back-office accounts appear in the leak. Henry and Adrian are 89% of all messages and their direct channel alone is 72%, so these five are support, not leadership.

  • Spectrum (337 msgs): US field operations, building infiltration, card skimmers, and roughly 10 to 15 field agents. English-speaking, US hours.
  • James (174 msgs): technical. Lookalike domains, server management, network-access buying, the rebrand, and a tool called "SpyCo."
  • Mike (53 msgs): sysadmin; procures infrastructure and accounts.
  • Monty (47 msgs): agent handler for vishing callers.
  • Dean (23 msgs): recruiting and enforcement; posted a kidnapping job at $50,000.

Field agents (Jevan 0026 Chicago, "caught"; Lex 0044 New York; David 0037 Los Angeles; others) are managed over Telegram and appear only as handles, not as accounts in the leak.

"Jamel Livingston." An external contact who negotiates with the forger FAKELAB and the exchanger Goldbear and tasks agents. Ransom-ISAC treats Jamel as an external fixer. Our read: more likely a Henry alias. All 46 occurrences are speaker lines Henry pastes from his own external DMs; no account ever references Jamel in the third person, and Adrian never names him, while the role matches Henry's exactly. Confidence: Possible, not established. The competing reading, a separate fixer only Henry relays, fits the same evidence, so it cannot be settled without sight of Henry's external accounts.

Zhenya: the Ruble-Side Launderer

Referred to as "Zhenya" (Женёк / Женька, a diminutive of Yevgeny). Confidence: role Credible, identity Unknown (pseudonym only).

  • Function: the crew's bitcoin-to-ruble cash-out and laundering service. He receives tainted BTC directly for cleaning and provides cash, with a minimum ticket ("he only does from 10k").
  • Trust: Adrian believes Zhenya skims the exchange rate ("he can accidentally say 0.3 where it is 0.13"). An external counterparty the crew does not fully trust.
  • Selectors: none. No surname, wallet, phone, or messenger handle for him appears in the leak. He is distinct from the US-facing exchangers Goldbear and @SAFUexchange: Zhenya is the ruble cash side.
  • Investigative value: any wallet that repeatedly receives from Henry's payout wallets and then moves to a ruble-converting desk is a Zhenya candidate. That attribution is a Data Solutions task.

Pipeline Mechanics

The crew runs victims through a fixed internal funnel, visible in their status postings. This vocabulary is not in public reporting and is useful for parsing the raw chat.

TermMeaning
CHATInitial contact established with the target
OFFERExtortion demand made
CONTRACTVictim engaged in negotiation
GOLDPaid. The crew's term for a completed extortion
TRG / SPR"Target" and "super target" (higher-value victim tier)

Settlement pattern: Adrian declares the GOLD, then requests Henry's wallet ("need your wallet, Brat"), and Henry supplies the receiving address. This holds across all 27 settlements and is the structural handle for wallet attribution.
